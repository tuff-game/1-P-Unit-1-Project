why jojo's bizzare adventures is the best anime

Plot:

This show is about an whole bloodline and every season/part is about a different generation. the main villan is the first main charcters adopted brother, dio. every part further down in the bloodline, the charicter always finds friends and goes on bizzare adventures to stop their parts villan

power system:

The power system is divided into 2 sections. for the first 2 parts, The power is something called hamon. it is a yellow lightning looking energy that Users use to punch through solid rock, run on water, heal wounds instantly, and slow their own aging. then in part 3 everything changes. a new power system comes about. It's called a stand. A stand is the embodiment of someone's soul/fighting spirit. you can be born with one but its extremely rare, and if you dont have one you can obtain one. to get a stand you have to acquire a stand you need to find a stand arrow which is super hard to do since anybody that doesn't have a stand, can't see them. so the general public doesn't know about stands. So if you somehow come across a stand arrow you have to stab yourself with it. if you're not worthy you die, but if you are worthy, you get a stand. stands can come in any shape or form. Some are humanoid, some are inanimate objects. They have different powers. Some can summon a magic beam and some can even stop time. Its very versatile and a super cool power system overall.
